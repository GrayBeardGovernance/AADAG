# AADAG Product Direction

**Two workbooks. One approach to AI decisions.**

Businesses are using AI, or thinking about it, but often need a practical way to decide where it fits.

AADAG will offer two workbooks. One helps a business examine AI it already uses. The other helps it decide where AI might be worth introducing.

Both start with the business decision, not the tool.

## Workbook 1: AI Implementation Assessment

**You already have AI. Is it working for your business?**

This workbook helps a business understand what its AI is doing, what decisions it influences, where people need to be involved, and what could work better.

Seven worksheets:

1. Current AI usage
2. Existing business decisions
3. AI authority today
4. Current risks and consequences
5. Human oversight and safeguards
6. Actual business value
7. Findings and improvement plan

The result is a practical picture of what's working, what needs adjusting, and what to do next.

## Workbook 2: AI Opportunity Assessment

**You're considering AI. Where should you start?**

This workbook helps a business identify worthwhile opportunities, think through the costs and expected return, and decide how much responsibility to give AI.

Seven worksheets:

1. Business needs
2. Potential AI opportunities
3. Proposed AI authority
4. Expected risks and consequences
5. Human oversight design
6. Projected costs, benefits, and ROI
7. Findings and implementation plan

The result is a grounded recommendation about where AI could help and what it would take to use it well.

## What Both Workbooks Share

Both use AADAG's decision path:

**Decision → Consequence → AI Influence → Human Authority**

They also ask what safeguards are needed, what evidence matters, and when a person needs to step in or stop the process.

Each workbook should include a completed example, plain-language instructions, and a findings page that turns the answers into an action plan.

## How They Become Products

The workbooks can be sold individually or as a bundle.

They can also support paid AADAG assessments, where a client wants someone to work through the questions, interpret the results, and help decide what comes next.

A future practitioner edition could give other consultants the worksheets, reporting templates, and guidance they need to use AADAG with their own clients.

## What We Build First

Start with the **AI Implementation Assessment Workbook**. Build and test its seven worksheets against a realistic business example.

Then build the **AI Opportunity Assessment Workbook** using the same foundation.

These are the planned products. The worksheets, examples, pricing, and licensing terms still need to be developed.
